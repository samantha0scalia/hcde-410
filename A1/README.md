**Data** This project uses Burke-Gilman Trail traffic counter north of NE 70th St 2019.

## Questions I asked

Q1. When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest? 
Q2. Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day?
Q3. Commuting or recreation? Is there evidence people use the Burke-Gilman to commute, not just for fun? Compare weekdays vs. weekends.
Q4. Are mornings mostly one direction and evenings the other? What would that tell you about who's using the trail and where they're heading?
